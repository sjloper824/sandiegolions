# Signage

Signage is seen from across a field, so it uses the fewest elements at the biggest sizes: logo, club name, one line and one colour field. See Components › Signage.

## Before ordering anything

- **Get vector logo files.** We only have PNGs, which blur at tent and banner size. Ask the board or the original designer for SVG, AI, EPS or PDF versions of the lion lockup and crest.
- Ask the printer for a digital proof, and for a printed colour proof on large or repeat orders. Fabric and vinyl shift maroon toward brown or purple.
- Use the print colours in the Flyers section and give the printer the Pantone references.

## Distance legibility

Allow about 1 inch of capital-letter height for every 10 feet of viewing distance.

| Seen from | Minimum letter height |
|---|---|
| 10 ft (table, A-frame) | 1 in |
| 30 ft (tent at a field) | 3 in |
| 60 ft (across the pitch) | 6 in |
| 100 ft (from the parking lot) | 10 in |

Set signage in Anton for words and in the logo artwork for the club name. Don't use Work Sans below 2 in on signage, and don't use Permanent Marker at all.

## Rules

- Use one colour field per piece: maroon or white, with charcoal only as a strip. Yellow is for a thin rule or a single word, never a large field. It fades and reads as gold outdoors.
- Don't put photos on signage smaller than a back wall. They don't read at distance.
- Use large-format artwork at 100–150 dpi at full size, which is why vector logos matter.
- Keep a 2 in margin from every edge and seam. Fabric pieces wrap around frames.
