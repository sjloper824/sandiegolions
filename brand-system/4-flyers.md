# Flyers

Flyers recruit new players, promote socials and thank sponsors. They follow the post layout, adapted for paper. See Components › Flyer.

## Sizes

| Piece | Trim size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Flyer | 8.5 × 11 in (Letter) | Noticeboards, cafes, gyms |
| Poster | 11 × 17 in (Tabloid) | Bars, sponsor venues |
| Handout | 5.5 × 8.5 in (half Letter) | Events, tents |
| Card | 4 × 6 in | Recruitment drops, sponsor thank-you cards |

## Print setup

- Add 0.125 in of **bleed** on every side when colour runs to the edge. Keep text and logos 0.25 in inside the trim (the **safe zone**).
- Use photos at 300 dpi at final size. Our Drive JPGs are fine up to about 8 × 6 in.
- Export a print PDF (Canva: "PDF Print" with crop marks and bleed) in CMYK.
- Use a matte or satin 100 lb cover stock for posters and cards, and 80–100 lb text for flyers.

## Print colours

Screen colours shift on paper, especially maroon. Use these as starting values, then match a printed proof before a big order.

| Token | Screen hex | CMYK starting point | Closest Pantone (check a swatch book) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `maroon-600` | #7A2A45 | 0 / 66 / 43 / 52 | PMS 209 C |
| `maroon-700` | #5F2037 | 0 / 66 / 42 / 63 | PMS 7421 C |
| `yellow-500` | #F7BC3C | 0 / 24 / 76 / 3 | PMS 136 C |
| `charcoal` | #17110F | rich black 60 / 50 / 50 / 100 for large areas, 0 / 0 / 0 / 100 for small text | Black 6 C |
| `white` | #FFFFFF | paper | — |

The CMYK numbers are straight conversions from the screen colours. A printer's profile will adjust them, so always ask for a proof. The Pantone matches are the nearest standard colours, not official club values; confirm them against a physical swatch before spot-colour jobs such as screen printing.

## Flyer layout (Letter)

1. Top band: the lion lockup top-left on white, with an eyebrow such as "SAN DIEGO LIONS · EST. 1997" beside or under it.
2. Headline: Anton, uppercase, 72–96pt, two to three lines, with the accent word in `maroon-600`.
3. One framed club photo: white frame, slight tilt.
4. Details panel: a rounded `maroon-600` or `charcoal` block (0.25 in corners) with Days, Time and Where in small uppercase yellow labels over white Anton values.
5. Call to action: a yellow box with charcoal text ("Come to training", "RSVP"), plus a QR code.
6. Footer line: sandiegolions.com · SanDiegoLionsAFC@gmail.com · "A 501(c)3 nonprofit". Add sponsor logos only on sponsor or event flyers.

## QR codes

- Point a QR code at a specific page (`sandiegolions.com/schedule.html`, `/contact.html`), not the homepage.
- Print it at least 1 × 1 in, black on white, with a white margin of at least 0.15 in. Never colour it maroon or put it on a photo.
- Test it from a printed proof with two different phones.
