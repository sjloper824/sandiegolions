The three approved club marks, as PNGs with transparent backgrounds. Use these files; never redraw or retype a logo.

- `clean-logo.png`: lion lockup, the everyday logo. Gold lion, maroon "LIONS", navy "SAN DIEGO" and "AUSTRALIAN FOOTBALL CLUB". Knock out to all white on maroon or charcoal.
- `crest-logo.png`: the formal shield (navy border, white top, maroon base). White backgrounds only.
- `script-wordmark.png`: maroon script with yellow speed lines. Socials and merch only; place on white.

Vector versions (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) are still needed for signage and large print. Ask the board for the originals.
