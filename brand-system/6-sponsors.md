# Sponsors

Sponsors fund equipment, field permits, umpires and operating costs. Our job is to make them look good next to the club without letting their brand take over ours. Paul Fields, Director of Sponsorships, owns sponsor relationships.

Current sponsors: MVA Design & Supply, Fast Times, Sun Bum, Podium Nutrition, Destination Concepts and Blenders Eyewear.

## Sponsor logos

- Get each sponsor's logo as a vector file or high-resolution PNG, plus an all-white version for dark grounds.
- Show their logo in their own colours, at the version they supply. Never recolour it maroon or redraw it.
- Each logo sits in its own tile: a white tile with a 2px `line` border, `r-card` corners and equal padding. All tiles in a group are the same size, regardless of logo shape. On maroon or charcoal, use the sponsor's all-white logo with no tile.
- A sponsor's logo is never bigger than the club's on the same piece.
- Keep sponsor order consistent everywhere. Group by tier if the club sets tiers; within a tier, list alphabetically.

## Where sponsors appear

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| Website home | "Thanks to all our sponsors" logo tiles above the footer |
| Website sponsors page | One card per sponsor: logo tile, name, one sentence, link |
| Kit | Current playing jumpers carry sponsor logos (Podium, MVA Design & Supply, Blenders and others). Placement is set with each sponsor. |
| Social | One thank-you post per sponsor at signing, plus a season round-up carousel |
| Email | Sponsor strip above the footer of the monthly update |
| Signage | 2 × 6 ft sponsor banner at home games and events; optional strip on the tent back wall |

## Sponsor deck

The sponsor deck is a 16:9 slide deck built in Claude Slides or Canva with this guide's colours and fonts. Keep it to about 10 slides.

1. **Cover:** crest, "Partner with the San Diego Lions", a framed team photo, season year.
2. **Who we are:** est. 1997, USAFL club, 4× premierships (Div. 1, 2 & 4), 100+ active players and supporters, 15 life members.
3. **More than a team:** the social calendar (Australia Day, Pie Day, Crawfish Boil, Flip Flop Formal, AFL Grand Final Night) with a costume photo.
4. **Community:** the Big Brothers Big Sisters youth clinic.
5. **Our audience:** reach and audience facts, filled in only from the club's real numbers (followers, email list size, event attendance) or marked `[TO CONFIRM]`.
6. **Where your brand shows up:** kit, website, social, email and signage, each with a real photo or mock.
7. **Partnership tiers:** `[TIER NAMES, PRICES AND BENEFITS]` from the board. Never invent prices.
8. **Where the money goes:** equipment, field permits, umpires, operating costs. A 501(c)3, so contributions may be tax-deductible; donors should confirm with their own tax advisor.
9. **Current partners:** logo tiles.
10. **Let's talk:** Paul Fields, Director of Sponsorships, and SanDiegoLionsAFC@gmail.com.

Deck design:

- Title slides use a `maroon-600` ground with white Anton. Content slides are white.
- Use one idea per slide, an Anton headline, at most three short points and one framed photo.
- Big numbers are set in Anton in `maroon-600`, with uppercase labels.
- Don't use stock photos or clip-art icons.
