# Social

Instagram and Facebook are where most people meet the club. Posts follow the website: white-forward, a rounded maroon or charcoal panel, Anton headlines, one framed photo, yellow used sparingly. Build them in Canva from the templates shown in Components (Game day posts, Social event post).

## Sizes

| Format | Size (px) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram and Facebook feed (default) | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | The default for every post |
| Instagram grid preview | crops to 3:4 | Keep text and logos 60px in from the left and right edges |
| Square, for carousels | 1080 × 1080 | Use only for carousels that mix with square slides |
| Stories and Reels covers | 1080 × 1920 | Keep text out of the top 250px and bottom 340px |
| Facebook event cover | 1920 × 1005 | Event name and date only; details go in the event text |

Platform sizes change. Check them before a big campaign.

## Layout for a 1080 × 1350 post

- Outer margin is 60px. Panels have 36px corners (`r-module` scaled up 1.5×).
- Put the logo top-left or bottom-left at 90–110px tall, on white or knocked out to white.
- Put an eyebrow (Work Sans 800, 26px, letter-spaced, uppercase) above the headline.
- Set the headline in Anton, uppercase, 110–150px, two to three lines at most.
- Use one framed photo at most, with a 20px white frame, tilted about 1.5°.
- Use one call to action per post, in a yellow box with charcoal text or as a short line: "Link in bio", "RSVP in the event", "Training Tue & Thu, 5:30 PM".
- Keep it to about 25 words of text on the graphic. The rest goes in the caption.

## Game-day graphics

Every game gets the same three posts, so fans learn to read them at a glance.

1. **Next game** (two to three days before): "LIONS VS [OPPONENT]", date, first bounce time, venue. White ground, maroon panel with the matchup, framed photo. See Components › Game day posts.
2. **Team list** (day before, optional): numbered list of the 22 by position line in Work Sans on white, with a charcoal header bar. Names come from the coach. Never guess.
3. **Result** (same day): charcoal ground, both teams, AFL-format scores (`8.6 (54)` to `5.4 (34)`), "WIN", "LOSS" or "DRAW" in a yellow tag, and up to three goal kickers or best players. See Components › Game day posts.

Also: **Player spotlight** (photo, name, nickname, position, years at the club, one quote) and **Recruitment** ("No experience necessary", training days, "Link in bio").

## Social and event posts

Social events get equal billing with games. Use the maroon-700 panel, the script wordmark or one Permanent Marker accent, and a costume or party photo. State what, when, where and how to RSVP. See Components › Social event post.

Club events to template: Australia Day, Pie Day, Crawfish Boil, Flip Flop Formal, AFL Grand Final Night, Big Brothers Big Sisters clinic.

## Sponsor thank-yous

Use a white ground with the sponsor's logo in a white tile with a `line` border, their name in Anton, one sentence in our voice and the sponsor's handle in the caption. One sponsor per post. See the Sponsors section.

## Captions

- Write one or two short sentences in the club voice, then the details, then the call to action.
- Use up to two emoji, and only when they add meaning.
- Tag sponsors and venues.
- Suggested hashtags: `#SanDiegoLions #footy #AussieRules #USAFL #SanDiego`. Use three to five.
- Name players the way they want to be named; use nicknames only with their OK.
