Every section and page opens with an eyebrow, an Anton heading and a short yellow rule.

- **Eyebrow:** the `eyebrow` style, uppercase, `maroon-600` on white or `yellow-500` on maroon and charcoal. Two to four words that name the topic ("Training & Games", "Our history").
- **Heading:** the `section` style (or `page-title` and `hero` at the top of a page), always uppercase. One accent word or phrase may be `maroon-600`.
- **Rule:** 64×5px `yellow-500`, 20px above and below.
- **Lead:** optional, the `lead` style in `ink-soft`, at most about 65 characters wide.

Provide: the eyebrow, the heading and an optional lead. Write headings as short statements in the club voice ("Nobody gets here alone."), ending with a full stop.
