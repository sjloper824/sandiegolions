Permanent Marker is the club's handwritten wink. Use it at most once per page, post, flyer or email, for a short aside of two to five words.

- **Badge:** `yellow-500` box, `charcoal` text, `marker` style, tilted −2°, square corners.
- **Note:** `maroon-600` text alone on white, tilted −1.5°.

Never use it for headlines, body text, numbers, dates or anything a reader needs to act on. In email, only as a small image with alt text.
