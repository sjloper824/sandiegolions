Every club photo sits in a white frame: 10–12px of `frame` white, `r-frame` corners, the photo inside at `r-photo`, `shadow-frame`, tilted 1.4° (or −1.2° to alternate down a page).

- Default ratio is 4:3. Use 1:1 for small photos and 4:5 for social.
- Remove the tilt on phones and in email.
- Use one framed photo per section. Never stack text on the photo.
- When a real photo isn't available yet, use the placeholder: `maroon-50` fill, a camera icon and a label saying which shot is needed. Never use a stock image.

Provide: a real club photo (resized to at most 1600px) and alt text naming who is in it and what they're doing.
