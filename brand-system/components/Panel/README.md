The rounded panel is the site's big colour moment: a `maroon-600`, `maroon-700` or `charcoal` block with `r-module` corners (16px on phones), inset 12–32px from the page edge, never full-bleed.

- Inside: a two-column split, copy on one side and a framed photo on the other, or a row of stats.
- Text on the panel: white headings, `maroon-100` paragraphs, `yellow-500` eyebrow, ghost buttons with a white border, or one yellow primary.
- Use `maroon-700` for the social-club panel, `maroon-600` for stats and community, and `charcoal` for closing panels and schedule cards.
- Don't put two panels of the same colour back to back. Separate them with a white section.

Provide: eyebrow, heading, one to two short paragraphs, one action, one framed photo. Padding is 40–72px vertical by panel size.
