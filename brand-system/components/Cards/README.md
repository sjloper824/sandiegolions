Cards come in two kinds, both with `r-card` corners and a 40×5px `yellow-500` bar above the title.

- **Explainer card:** `charcoal` fill, white Anton title (`card-title`), body at 78% white. Use for "What is footy?" style facts, three to a row.
- **Link card:** white fill, 2px `charcoal` border, a small `maroon-600` uppercase label, Anton title, `ink-soft` text and a text link. Use for navigation (Meet the Board, Life Members), sponsors, board members and the next-game card.

Cards in a row share padding (24–32px) and height. Use three per row on desktop and one on phones. Never use a coloured left border, shadows or emoji on cards.
