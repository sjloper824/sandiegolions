Social events get the party version of the system: a full `maroon-700` ground, the script wordmark on a white tile, one Permanent Marker badge, a costume or party photo in a white frame, the event name in Anton and one yellow call to action.

- Feed post 1080 × 1350 with a 60px margin. Story 1080 × 1920 with nothing in the top 250px or bottom 340px.
- Headline: the event name and a short promise ("Pie Day is back."), at most two lines.
- Details in one line: day and date, time, venue. Cost and dress code go in the caption.
- Call to action: "RSVP in the event", "Link in bio" or "Tap to RSVP".

Provide: the event name, date, time, venue and RSVP route from the social committee, and a real photo from a past club event.
