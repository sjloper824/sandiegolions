# Email

Club emails go out through Mailchimp. Set the brand up once in Mailchimp as a saved template using the layout below, then build every email from it. See Components › Email template.

## Layout

- The body is 600px wide on a `white` background, with an outer background of `white` too. No grey frames.
- **Header:** the lion lockup on white, 64px tall, centred or left. Under it, a 4px `charcoal` rule.
- **Hero:** one club photo, full width of the content, 4:3 or 3:2. In email the photo has no tilt and no shadow; use 12px rounded corners where Mailchimp allows, square otherwise.
- **Headline:** Anton, uppercase, 36–40px, `ink`, with the eyebrow above it (13px, 800 weight, letter-spaced, `maroon-600`).
- **Body:** 16px, line height 1.6, `ink-soft`. Keep paragraphs to three lines.
- **Details block:** a `charcoal` box with 20px padding. Small uppercase yellow labels (Date, Time, Where) above white values.
- **Button:** a solid `yellow-500` box with `charcoal` text, 800 weight, uppercase, 2px corners, at least 44px tall. Use a real Mailchimp button block, not an image of a button. Use one primary button per email.
- **Footer:** a `maroon-600` block with white text: club name, "A 501(c)3 nonprofit", the club email, social links and Mailchimp's required mailing address and unsubscribe link.

## Fonts in email

Many email apps ignore web fonts. Set fallbacks so the email still looks right:

- Headlines: `Anton, Impact, "Arial Narrow", sans-serif`
- Body: `"Work Sans", Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`
- Don't use Permanent Marker as live text. If you want the handwritten accent, use it once as a small image with alt text.

## Email types

| Type | When | Headline example | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Season launch / recruitment | Before April | "No experience necessary." | Come to training |
| Event invite | 2–3 weeks before a social | "Pie Day is back." | RSVP |
| Club update / newsletter | Monthly in season | "Round [X] wrap-up" | See the schedule |
| Sponsor thank-you | After a sponsorship lands or at season end | "Thanks to our sponsors." | Meet our sponsors |

## Writing

- The subject line is under 45 characters, specific and friendly: "Pie Day is Saturday. Bring a fork."
- The preheader is one line that adds the key detail: date, time or place.
- Everything important goes in live text so it shows when images are blocked. Every image gets alt text.
- Test-send to Gmail on a phone and on desktop before every send.
