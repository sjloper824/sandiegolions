What is footy?

Fast, physical, and genuinely fun.

No sideline to hide on

Australian Football, or “footy,” is fast-paced and physical. There's no offside and a role for every build and skill set — kick, mark, handball, tackle.

Zero experience required

We'll teach you the rules and the skills from scratch. Come to a Tuesday or Thursday training session and we'll get you kicking a footy within the hour.

A club, not just a team

The Lions have sent multiple players to the U.S.A. Revolution, the Men's National Team, over the past 15 years — and we throw one of the best socials in San Diego club sport.

Off the field

We’re a social club too.

Sport's not the only reason people stick around. The Lions run one of the best social calendars in San Diego club sport — Australia Day, Pie Day, the Crawfish Boil, the Flip Flop Formal, and AFL Grand Final Night, to name a few.

Come for the footy. Stay for everything else.

We care about our community

The Lions have partnered with San Diego Big Brothers Big Sisters to bring Australian football to local youth aged 8–15, teaching fundamentals, teamwork, and the simple fact that sport is more fun when everyone gets a game.

It's how we build the next generation of players — and one of our favorite days on the calendar.
